welcome to the project

Kevin Priestley 14 November 2026

this website is an ongoing project. a place away from social media where i can write about... whatever i want. i intend to focus this space on music, art, and daily life as a dad-musician. i'll try not to over-do it, but it's unambigously me to write about politics and philosophy. eventually, i'll create tags and make it easier to search for stuff on the site. 

if you want updates directly to your inbox, fill out this sign-up form. 

much love.

- Kevin 

p.s. i take data privacy really seriously, so this site doesn't have tracking enabled and the sign-up form info you submit is hosted in the EU, where the information you share falls under GDPR guidelines.
